## Next company

What are your expectations for your next company?

I’m looking for a company with a strong engineering culture, where code quality, system design, and best practices are taken seriously.

I want to work on scalable systems where I can contribute my experience and make a real impact on the product.

I also value a collaborative environment where team members support each other, share knowledge, and communicate openly.

At the same time, I’m looking to grow both technically and professionally, and also contribute back to the team by mentoring junior developers.

## New environment

How do you feel when working in a new environment

When working in a new environment, I usually feel both excited and challenged.

Excited because it’s an opportunity to learn new technologies, understand a different business domain, and work with new people.

At the same time, I also see it as a challenge, especially in the beginning when I need to quickly adapt to new processes, systems, and ways of working.

To handle that, I focus on learning as much as possible early on — understanding the system, asking questions, and aligning with teammates and Business Analysts to get a clear picture of how things work.

I also try to contribute as soon as possible, even with small tasks at first, so I can build confidence and deliver value quickly.

I believe that a new environment is a great opportunity to grow, as long as I stay proactive and open to learning.

## Ideal environment

What does an ideal work environment look like for you?

For me, an ideal work environment is one where there is a strong engineering culture, with a focus on code quality, system design, and best practices.

I also value a collaborative environment where team members support each other, share knowledge, and communicate openly.

Clear requirements and good alignment with Business Analysts and stakeholders are also very important to me, because they help ensure we are building the right solutions.

In addition, I prefer an environment that encourages continuous learning and improvement, where I can grow technically and also contribute back to the team, for example through mentoring or sharing knowledge.

I believe an ideal environment is one where both individuals and the team can grow together while delivering high-quality and meaningful products.

## Independent or collaborative

How do you prefer to work — independently or collaboratively?

I’m comfortable working both independently and collaboratively /kəˈlæb.ɚ.ə.t̬ɪv.li/, depending on the situation.

When working independently, I can stay focused and take ownership of tasks, especially when implementing features or solving technical problems.

At the same time, I really value collaboration, particularly when it comes to clarifying requirements, discussing design decisions, or solving complex issues. Working with teammates and Business Analysts helps ensure we’re aligned and building the right solution.

In practice, I usually combine both — I collaborate early to align on the approach, then work independently to execute, and reconnect with the team when needed.

I believe the key is not choosing one over the other, but knowing how to balance both to achieve the best outcome.

## Why join

What do you know about our company, and why do you want to join us?

From what I know, KMS Technology is well-known for its strong engineering culture and high-quality software development practices. I also understand that the company works on both product development and software services, which gives engineers the opportunity to work on diverse and meaningful projects.

I’m particularly interested in how KMS focuses on building scalable systems and encourages best practices in software engineering.

What attracts me most is the opportunity to work in an environment where quality, collaboration, and continuous improvement are highly valued.

This aligns well with my goal of growing deeper in system design and building scalable, maintainable systems, especially in a professional and engineering-driven environment.

I believe KMS would be a great place for me to both contribute my experience and continue developing toward a more senior and technical leadership role.

## Tech stack

What are your thoughts on our tech stack/product?

From what I understand, your tech stack and products are built with a strong focus on scalability, maintainability, and real-world usability.

I also see that KMS emphasizes modern engineering practices and supports building robust systems, especially through your product platforms like Katalon and Kobiton.

What I find interesting is the focus on solving real customer problems with practical and scalable solutions, rather than overcomplicating the system.

This aligns well with my own approach, where I focus on building clean, maintainable systems and choosing the right level of complexity based on actual needs.

I believe this kind of engineering mindset is important for building high-quality products that can evolve over time.

## International teammates

What are your thoughts on working with teammates from different countries?

I see working with teammates from different countries as a great opportunity. It brings diverse perspectives, which often lead to better ideas and more well-rounded solutions.

Of course, there can be challenges, such as differences in communication styles, time zones, or cultural expectations. But I think those can be managed with clear communication and mutual /ˈmjuː.tʃu.əl/ respect.

In my experience, I try to be mindful of these differences—for example, communicating more clearly, confirming understanding, and being flexible with collaboration.

Overall, I believe working in a diverse team helps me grow both professionally and personally, and it also improves the quality of the product we build.
