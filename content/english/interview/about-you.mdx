## Duties at ONPOINT

What duties have you been responsible for in your current role at your company ONPOINT?

In my current role, I’m mainly responsible for two areas: **fraud detection and return management**.

My company operates online stores for international consumer brands on platforms like Shopee, TikTok, and Lazada. Returns are a high cost for the business. Most customers are genuine, but some customers abuse the return system. For example, they may claim that an expensive item never arrived, return an empty box, or create multiple accounts using the same phone number or address.

My first responsibility is **fraud detection**. I work with the operations and finance teams to define rules for identifying suspicious behavior. For example, we look at customers with very high return rates, repeated “item not received” claims, or multiple accounts sharing the same information.

Then I turn these business rules into system logic. When a return comes in, the system automatically checks the case and gives our team a recommendation, such as approving the return or holding it for further review.

My second responsibility is the **decision and enforcement flow**. When a fraud case is confirmed, the system can reject the refund. If the customer continues the same behavior, we can also block them from placing new orders, especially cash-on-delivery orders, because they have a higher risk.

I also make sure that every decision is properly recorded, including who approved or rejected the case. This makes the whole process traceable and easier to explain to the brand later.

The main goal is to reduce fraud and unnecessary costs for the brands. At the same time, we don’t want to make the process difficult for genuine customers. By filtering out risky cases, our team can process normal returns faster and give genuine customers a better experience.

So overall, my responsibility is to build a system that is strong enough to prevent abuse, but simple enough that it doesn’t create unnecessary problems for honest customers.
