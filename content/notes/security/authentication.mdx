<ArticleStack>

- Authentication is the process of verifying the identity of an user in a system
- To confirm the identity of the user or system and establish trust.

<Divided>

## Methods

1. **Username and Password:** Traditional method where users provide a unique identifier (username) and a secret credential (password).
2. **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** Involves using multiple forms of identification, such as passwords, security tokens, biometrics, etc.
3. **Biometric Authentication:** Uses physical or behavioral characteristics like fingerprints, iris scans, or voice recognition.

## Example

When you log in to an online banking system, you provide your username and password. The system verifies these credentials to confirm your identity.
