<ArticleStack>

- Authorization is the process of granting or denying access rights and permissions to authenticated users or systems.
- To control what actions or resources an authenticated user or system can access.

<Divided>

## Methods

1. **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Assigns permissions based on roles, where users are assigned roles with corresponding access rights.
2. **Attribute-Based Access Control (ABAC):** Access decisions are based on attributes of the user, the resource, and the environment.
3. **Discretionary Access Control (DAC):** Allows resource owners to determine who can access their resources and what actions they can perform.

## Example

After successfully logging in, an authenticated user may be authorized to view certain pages, edit their profile, but not access administrative functions.
