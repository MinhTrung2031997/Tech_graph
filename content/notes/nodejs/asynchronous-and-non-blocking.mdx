<ArticleStack>
  <SplitPair>
    <SplitCol title="Asynchronous">

**Asynchronous** means the result is returned later (via callback/Promise), not immediately.

</SplitCol>
    <SplitCol title="Non-blocking">

**Non-blocking** means the program does not pause execution while waiting for the result.

</SplitCol>
  </SplitPair>
  <Divided>

**Asynchronous improves efficiency** by allowing other tasks to run during waiting time, not by making the task itself faster.

</Divided>
</ArticleStack>
