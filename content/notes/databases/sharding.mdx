**Sharding** is the technique of splitting a large database into **smaller, independent pieces** called **shards**.

Each shard contains a **subset of the data**, and all shards together form the complete database.

Sharding is used when a single SQL database becomes too:

- slow
- large
- overloaded
- expensive
- difficult to scale vertically
